## Snap template, packages and tooling

The project starts from Lens Studio's **SPECS Base Template** and retains native Snap material, package metadata and references, including SpectaclesInteractionKit, SpectaclesUIKit, Leaf, Motion, Icons, Bitmoji 3D, rendering resources, the vertex-color shader/material package and generated IconAtlas editor tooling. The runtime kit typography is part of that pre-existing package content.

Those components remain subject to their respective Snap-supplied licenses, notices and platform terms. They are not relicensed by the Inter, Apache or MIT notices above, and are not claimed as original FoldSpace work. Native package files and metadata are preserved for reproducibility.

## Newly generated project assets

The FoldSpace paper mesh is authored in TypeScript. The click, paper and flight WAVs were synthesized locally with CLAD's algorithmic sound workflow. No third-party samples, music track or paper-airplane model was incorporated into those new assets.
